About the Clinic

About Dr. Shaikh's Clinic

Dr. Shaikh's Clinic (शेख क्लिनिक) is a general practitioner healthcare facility serving patients in the Chilkalthana and Jadhavwadi area of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra.

The clinic is committed to providing accessible, patient-centred medical consultation in a comfortable and professional environment. Patients are seen with care and clear communication throughout the consultation process.

Located at Sunny Centre in the MIDC Industrial Area of Chilkalthana, the clinic is conveniently accessible for residents of the surrounding community.

Sunny Centre, opposite New Mondha Road, Jadhavwadi, MIDC Industrial Area, Chilkalthana, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra 431008
